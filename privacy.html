Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This app is a personal setup of rclone, used only by its owner, Steven Toews, with his own Google account.

What it accesses

Files in the owner's Google Drive, which rclone copies to the owner's own computer.

What it collects or shares

Nothing. No data is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and nothing is used for any purpose other than copying the owner's own files.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
